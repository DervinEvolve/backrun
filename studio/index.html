What is recorded and what is staging

Recorded in the run folder: every stroke's path, width, water and pigments, the pigment each stroke added, the wet area after each op, the candidates the painter rejected, the order of ops, the dries, the recorded time and the sheet frames.

Staging: the dips, rinses, brush changes, the panel's ride to the drying rack and how long each op lasts on screen are choreography derived from the ledger. Frames were saved every 20 simulator steps, so between two frames the sheet blends toward the next frame under a mask that follows the brush.
